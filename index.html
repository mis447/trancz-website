About Trancz Fashion

Built on Craft,
Driven by Vision

Trancz Fashion, based in Mumbai, India, is a leading garment manufacturing company specializing in premium apparel production, fabric sourcing, and private label solutions. Founded by Rohit Jain, Trancz is a GST-registered manufacturer delivering design-driven clothing for domestic brands and businesses.

We offer complete FOB and DDP solutions — from concept and pattern design to stitching, finishing, and packaging — for fashion, lifestyle, and corporate clients.

What is your minimum order quantity?+
Our low MOQ starts from 1,000 pieces, making us ideal for startups and emerging brands. We offer flexible style and size combinations within this limit.
What garment types do you manufacture?+
T-shirts, polo shirts, hoodies, caps, and more. We specialize in streetwear, fast-fashion, and corporate apparel including private label and corporate gifting products.
Do you offer private label manufacturing?+
Yes — complete private label solutions including design, fabric sourcing, production, branding, and packaging, all under one roof.
What printing techniques do you offer?+
HD Print, PUFF, DTF (Direct to Film), Foil printing, Spray, screen printing, and digital textile printing — all handled by our in-house karigars.
What fabrics can you source?+
Cotton and performance textiles. We also collaborate with mills to develop custom fabrics tailored to your brand's requirements.
Where are you located?+
We are based in Dombivli, Mumbai, Maharashtra, India, operating from a 4,500 sq.ft. facility with 35 modern machines and 32 team members.
