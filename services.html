Sampling & Prototype Development
High-quality garment samples before bulk production to finalize fits, fabrics, and design details. Complete accuracy and client approval at every step — no surprises in bulk.
Pattern Making
Precise master patterns based on tech packs and references, ensuring accurate sizing and fit across all styles. Our experienced team lays the foundation for efficient, error-free production.
Low MOQ Manufacturing for Startups
We support emerging brands with completely flexible order quantities, tailored to their specific needs. Choose the styles, sizes, and quantities that work for you — without rigid minimums or large commitments.
Embroidery Services
Professional embroidery solutions that bring your brand to life with precision and detail. From logos and lettering to custom designs, we deliver clean, durable embroidery tailored to your garments and brand identity.
Fabric Sourcing & Development
Premium fabrics — Cotton, Poly, Lyrca, Suede, Corduroy, Anti-pilling, Twill, Brushing checks, Drill fleece and performance textiles. We collaborate with mills to develop custom fabrics tailored to your brand's specific requirements.
Custom Garment Printing
HD, DTF, puff, and foil printing on the garments we manufacture for you — done in-house as part of your order, not offered as a standalone printing service.
